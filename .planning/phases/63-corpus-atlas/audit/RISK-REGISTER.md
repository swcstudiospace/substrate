## Reading the scores

- The highest score is RK10 (9): the disk was 98 percent full and 106 worktree directories exist. It needs the owner's gate (e) before any removal.
- Scores of 6 are RK1, RK3, RK4, RK7, RK9, RK11 and RK12. RK9 and RK1 are the two that could cause irreversible harm (a deleted document, a leaked one); both are fail-closed by design.
- RK5 and RK6 are low by design: the chain is a witness and naive mode builds no graph.
- Likelihood is a judgement from the scout evidence, not a measurement. No risk was tested live.
