# Plan 08.1-01: Version conflicts carry the standing version

**Objective:** a rewrite of a decision works however many newer entries share its scope. The gate reports the version in force on versioned conflicts (`current_version`), and the swarm client resubmits from it without searching. This closes the MEM-02 gap found after the v1.1 audit: `memory_search` matches only the body, and a decision behind 100+ newer entries could not be rewritten.

## Contract
- `MemoryWriteResult.current_version?: number | null`.
- It is present exactly when the outcome is `conflict`, the reason is one of `version.required`, `version.stale`, `version.raced` or `standing.raced`, the conflict plan resolves `versioned` (a decision with a subject), and the lookup of the entry in force succeeded.
- The value is the `version` of the decision now in force for the scope and subject, or `null` when none is in force.
- It is absent on every other conflict (`review.required`, `write.in-flight`, non-versioned `standing.raced`, ...) and when the lookup failed.

## Tasks

### Task 1 (agent-substrate): `current_version` on versioned conflicts
- `packages/mcp-server/src/types.ts`: add `current_version?: number | null` to `MemoryWriteResult` with a doc comment that states the contract above.
- `packages/mcp-server/src/memory-write.ts`: set it on the `version.required`, `version.stale`, `version.raced` and `standing.raced` replies when the plan resolves `versioned`. The value comes from the standing entry or race winner the gate already holds, or from a lookup of the entry in force. Use `null` when none is in force. Omit the key when the lookup failed and on every other conflict.
- `packages/mcp-server/src/memory-write.test.ts`: test each of the four reasons (value present, `null` when nothing stands) and that the key is absent on `review.required`, `write.in-flight`, non-versioned `standing.raced` and when the lookup fails.
- `packages/mcp-server/src/index-store.memory.test.ts` (real Postgres, `SUBSTRATE_TEST_PG_URL`): a decision with subject `db choice` and body `use postgres 16`, with 120 newer entries in its `repo:` scope. A rewrite without `expected_version` gives `version.required` with `current_version: 1`. A resubmit with `expected_version: 1` is accepted at v2, and v1 is superseded.
- `docs/memory-governance.md`: document `current_version` among the conflict reply fields.

### Task 2 (agent-swarm): the client uses `current_version`
- `swarm/memory.py`: on a `version.*` / `standing.raced` conflict for a decision with a subject, check the reply for `current_version`:
  - an int (not a bool): resubmit with `expected_version` set to it;
  - `null`: resubmit without `expected_version`;
  - in both cases, make no `memory_search` call;
  - key absent (an older server): fall back to `_current_version`, with its behaviour unchanged.
  - `MAX_VERSION_RESUBMITS` and the same `idempotency_key` apply on every path.
- `tests/test_swarm_memory.py`: the fake `memory_search` matches honestly, so it no longer finds a decision by subject when the subject is not in the body. Add tests for the int path, the `null` path, a bool rejected as a version, no `memory_search` call on either path, the absent-key fallback, the resubmit cap and the reused key.
- `docs/substrate-memory.md`: the conflict handler reads `current_version` first and re-reads only against older servers.

### Task 3 (live): regression on the real gate
- Run substrate-mcp from the fix branch with a real Postgres and the swarm client from the fix branch.
- Seed a decision with subject `db choice` and body `use postgres 16` at `repo:acme/scratch`, then 120 newer facts in that scope.
- Rewrite the decision through `swarm/memory.py` without `expected_version`.
- Expected: accepted, v2 standing, v1 superseded, and no `memory_search` call (2 POSTs, 0 searches).

## Verification
- agent-substrate: `bunx tsc --noEmit`, and `bun test` with `SUBSTRATE_TEST_PG_URL` set (live Postgres), 0 fail.
- agent-swarm: `ruff check` (only the pre-existing E401 findings in `tests/test_uplift_harness.py`), `pytest`, and `build_agents --check` and `build_trae_agents --check` up to date.
- Live run (task 3): the decision behind 120 newer entries is rewritten to v2, v1 is superseded, and no `memory_search` call is made.

## Success criteria
1. Versioned conflicts (`version.required`, `version.stale`, `version.raced`, `standing.raced` on a decision with a subject) carry `current_version`. Every other conflict does not.
2. The swarm client resubmits from `current_version` without searching, and falls back to the re-read only when the key is absent.
3. A decision behind 100+ newer entries in its scope is rewritten on the real gate.

## Out of scope
- `memory_search` semantics (body-only matching and the 100-entry cap are unchanged).
- New conflict reasons, new event kinds, other gate behaviour.
