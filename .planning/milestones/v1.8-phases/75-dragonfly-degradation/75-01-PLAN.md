Decisions D75-1 to D75-4 in `75-CONTEXT.md`. No Railway write, no production Dragonfly, no port 7410. A live cache-plane proof is operator-gated and is not a task.

Store of truth for the answer is the index (brief) and Hindsight (recall), not Dragonfly (D75-3). `budget` and `gen` are cache key classes, not admission classes (D75-2).

Shadow recording (resolves the Flags sentence against residual R3): `fail-closed` in `shadow` records `bypass` and never refuses. It does not record `shadow_limited`. A cache outage is the breaker's signal, not a budget breach, so `refused` stays out of the admission SLO numerator in `packages/observability/src/slo.ts`.

## Tasks

- **T1 Flags (D75-1, D75-2, D75-2a).** In `packages/mcp-server/src/config.ts` `loadAdmissionSettings`, parse `SUBSTRATE_BUDGET_READ_BREAKER`, `SUBSTRATE_BUDGET_WRITE_BREAKER`, and `SUBSTRATE_BUDGET_COORDINATION_BREAKER` through `breakerPolicyOf`. Unset or blank is `fail-open`. `fail-open` and `fail-closed` (trimmed, case-insensitive) are the only other values. Any other value throws `ConfigError` naming the variable, and `loadConfig` calls `loadAdmissionSettings` unguarded so boot refuses. Do not change `SUBSTRATE_BUDGETS` (it still falls back to `shadow`). Register the family `SUBSTRATE_BUDGET_<CLASS>_BREAKER` in `packages/mcp-server/src/flag-registry.ts` (`CLASS_BREAKER_NAMES`, members from `ADMISSION_CLASSES` only). Hand-render the same row in `docs/config-reference.md` and the flag table in `docs/observability.md`. Files: `packages/mcp-server/src/config.ts`, `packages/mcp-server/src/flag-registry.ts`, `docs/config-reference.md`, `docs/observability.md`.
- **T2 Admission and cache fallback (D75-2, D75-3, D75-4).** In `packages/mcp-server/src/admission.ts`, an open-breaker refusal (`ScriptOutcome.breakerRetryAfterMs` set, only when `callPlane` refused before sending a command) on a `fail-closed` class in `enforce` returns decision `refused` with the breaker's wait. `fail-open`, and `fail-closed` in `shadow`, return `bypass` and never refuse. A timeout, connect error, or malformed reply stays `bypass` in every class. `budgetBody` uses `error: "cache_unavailable"` for `refused` and `error: "budget"` for `limited`. `isRefusal` treats `refused` like `limited`. In `packages/mcp-server/src/server.ts`, `sendBudgetRefusal` answers 503 with `Retry-After` for `refused` and 429 for `limited`, on both the REST admission path and the MCP `admitBatch` path. Do not add a flag for generation keys (D75-4): a refused `bump` stays in `staleGenerations` in `packages/mcp-server/src/cache.ts`, and `getOrLoad` bypasses that key until a successful bump. Brief (`service.ts` `cache.getOrLoad` klass `brief`) and recall (`recallUncounted` klass `memory-search`) keep their existing `load` as the answer; a refused cache read falls through. Do not add `refused` to the SLO numerator in `packages/observability/src/slo.ts` (R3). Cross-process staleness after a refused bump (R1) is out of scope. Files: `packages/mcp-server/src/admission.ts`, `packages/mcp-server/src/server.ts`, `packages/mcp-server/src/cache.ts`, `packages/observability/src/slo.ts`.
- **T3 Tests.** `packages/mcp-server/src/cache-breaker.test.ts`: open the cache breaker with a `PlaneRegistry` gate and no live Redis; brief answered by the index; recall equal to the uncached reply; generation keys stay on the source after the breaker closes until a bump lands; admission over the gated cache (`fail-closed` refused, `fail-open` bypass, no breaker failure recorded for the refusals). Run brief and recall with no flags set (D75-1). `packages/mcp-server/src/admission.test.ts`: default `loadAdmissionSettings({})` is all `fail-open`; enforce `refused`; shadow `bypass` (not `shadow_limited`); non-breaker failure stays `bypass`; batch body `cache_unavailable`; flag parsing (valid, blank is `fail-open`, invalid throws `ConfigError` naming the variable and `loadConfig` throws). `packages/mcp-server/src/http.admission.test.ts`: fail-open admits (200); fail-closed answers 503 with `Retry-After`. Leave "a harness with no cache at all" and "a dead cache admits" unchanged. Files: `packages/mcp-server/src/cache-breaker.test.ts`, `packages/mcp-server/src/admission.test.ts`, `packages/mcp-server/src/http.admission.test.ts`.
- **T4 Docs.** In `docs/observability.md`, next to the cache breaker paragraph: the three flags, that unset means today's `fail-open` bypass, that `fail-closed` is 503 `cache_unavailable` only in `enforce`, that shadow records `bypass` and never refuses, that brief and recall fall through to the source, and that a refused generation bump is in-process only (R1, 5-minute brief TTL). Do not document a production cutover. Files: `docs/observability.md`, `docs/config-reference.md`.
- **T5 Gates (main agent).** `bunx tsc --noEmit` in `packages/mcp-server` and `packages/observability`; `bun test` on the three test files in T3. No disposable Redis and no Railway.
