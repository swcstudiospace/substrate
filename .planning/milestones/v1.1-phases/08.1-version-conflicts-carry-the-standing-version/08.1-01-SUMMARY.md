# Plan 08.1-01 Summary

## Gap
MEM-02, found after the v1.1 audit (which had marked it satisfied). The swarm client learned a decision's version by searching memory. `memory_search` matches only entry bodies (`to_tsvector('english', body)`, `packages/mcp-server/src/index-store.ts:1185`) and returns at most the 100 newest entries per scope. The conflict reply named the standing entry (`conflict_with`) but not its version. The phase 8 swarm test `test_standing_decision_beyond_the_scope_page_is_found_by_the_targeted_read` passed only because its fake answered subject queries.

## What changed
- **agent-substrate #13** (`fix/version-conflict-current-version`; 3e08f90 planning insert, 5cd8318 fix). `MemoryWriteResult` gains `current_version?: number | null`. A conflict on a versioned decision (`version.required`, `version.stale`, `version.raced`, `standing.raced`) carries the version in force now, or `null` when none is in force; the key is absent when the gate's lookup failed. Helpers `inForce`, `currentVersion`, `resubmitAgainst` in `memory-write.ts`. Unit tests in `memory-write.test.ts`; real-gate test in `index-store.memory.test.ts`. `docs/memory-governance.md` documents the field.
- **agent-swarm #8** (`fix/memory-version-from-conflict`; c7909d0). `swarm/memory.py` resubmits from `current_version` (int -> `expected_version`; `null` -> without it) and makes no `memory_search` call. The re-read stays only as the fallback for older servers, its limit documented. Test fake searches bodies only, newest first, capped. New tests: int/null/non-int values, the older-server limit, the resubmit cap. `docs/substrate-memory.md` updated.
