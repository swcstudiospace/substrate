## Residual risks

- GreptimeDB rehearsal of `planning.transition` has not run (operator-gated; ROADMAP.md phase 54 status says the same). The in-repo proof is the sink unit tests and the observer fake-sink tests above, not a live write.
- Failed heartbeats are not shown as DEGRADED in the session view. A failed write cannot be read by the reader, and no reader-visible state carries it; the reader-visible states are STALE and ERROR, so a session whose write failed shows STALE once its heartbeat leaves the freshness window. Recorded here, not implemented. (Carried forward from prior verifications; still true on this tree.)
- LOADING is reachable only before the first read: only `deriveSessionState` returns LOADING (`states.ts:47`) and the reader always passes `loaded: true` (`read.ts:224-232`). A view that never completes its first read stays LOADING by construction.
- A change that lands during a shared scan is recorded by the next scan, one tick later (single-flight scan in `observer.ts`); delayed, not dropped.
- `unpublishedOutcome` counts added and changed paths only (`observer.ts:870-873`); removals in an incomplete diff are not counted.
- Records still in flight after the shutdown bound may not be delivered before exit; past `MAX_PENDING_TRANSITIONS` a record goes to the fallback alone (`observer.ts:482-485`).
