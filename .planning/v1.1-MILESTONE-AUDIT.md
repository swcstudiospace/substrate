## Post-audit finding (closed)

The first audit marked MEM-02 satisfied on the strength of a test whose fake answered subject queries the real server cannot. The gap was found after that audit.

- **Finding.** The swarm memory client learned a decision's version by searching memory. The substrate's `memory_search` matches only entry bodies (`to_tsvector('english', body)`, `packages/mcp-server/src/index-store.ts:1185`) and returns at most the 100 newest entries per scope. The gate's conflict reply named the standing entry (`conflict_with`) but not its version. `test_standing_decision_beyond_the_scope_page_is_found_by_the_targeted_read` passed only because its fake found decisions by subject.
- **Live reproduction.** On the merged mains (agent-substrate 7eba2fc, agent-swarm 8c11231), real Postgres + GreptimeDB: a decision with subject `db choice` and body `use postgres 16`, then 120 newer facts in `repo:acme/scratch`. The rewrite without `expected_version` ended `status=conflict reason=version.required` after 1 POST and 2 searches, and the decision stayed at v1.
- **Closure.** Phase 8.1 (gap closure, `08.1-01-PLAN.md`) was inserted:
  - agent-substrate #13 (`fix/version-conflict-current-version`; 3e08f90 planning insert, 5cd8318 fix): a conflict on a versioned decision (`version.required`, `version.stale`, `version.raced`, `standing.raced`) carries `current_version`, the version in force now, or `null` when none; the key is absent when the gate's lookup failed. Files: `types.ts`, `memory-write.ts`, `memory-write.test.ts`, `index-store.memory.test.ts`, `docs/memory-governance.md`.
  - agent-swarm #8 (`fix/memory-version-from-conflict`; c7909d0): the client resubmits from `current_version` (int -> `expected_version`; `null` -> without it) with no `memory_search`. The re-read stays only as the fallback for older servers, with its limit documented. The test fake searches bodies only (newest first, capped). New tests cover int, `null` and non-int values, the older-server limit and the resubmit cap. Files: `swarm/memory.py`, `tests/test_swarm_memory.py`, `docs/substrate-memory.md`.
- **Verification.**
  - agent-substrate: `bunx tsc --noEmit` clean; `bun test` with live Postgres 1003 pass / 0 fail (998 before). The real-gate test "a version conflict names the version in force, however buried the decision is" passes: a decision behind 120 newer entries gives a conflict with `current_version` 1, and a resubmit with the same key is accepted at v2.
  - agent-swarm: full pytest passes; `tests/test_swarm_memory.py` passed 3 of 3 runs; ruff reports only the two pre-existing E401 findings (`tests/test_uplift_harness.py`); `build_agents.py --check` and `build_trae_agents.py --check` up to date; `bun test tests/ts` 1 pass.
  - Live, both fixes: the rewrite behind 120 newer entries was accepted after 2 POSTs and 0 searches; v1 superseded, v2 in force.
  - Live, fixed client against the unfixed server: still ends in conflict `version.required` after 1 POST and 2 searches. It fails safe; this is the documented older-server limit.
