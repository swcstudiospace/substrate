End-to-end flows:

1. **Swarm plan -> bind -> tee -> verify -> another surface's brief** (S1). The real `orch_plan` ran on a scratch repo:
   - It bound `smoke-corr-1` to a `ut-…` Graph ID and registered 13 nodes under `acme/scratch` as `swarm-a01-orch`.
   - The teed events landed under that Graph ID as `swarm-a01-orch` with session `A01@r0:<graph>`. The binding note has its own session, `bind:<graph>:<corr>`.
   - `events_verify` returned `{ok:true, events:3}`.
   - A `claude-code` `/brief` for `acme/scratch` listed the `swarm-a01-orch` actions and the open swarm graph.
   - A re-plan reused the binding. With substrate-mcp stopped, `orch_plan` still exited 0. No token leaked into the swarm output.
2. **ultrathink plan note under the same Graph ID** (S1). The live server accepted claude-ultrathink's `emitEvent`, and its `ultrathink planned graph …` note is in the ledger under the swarm run's Graph ID.
3. **Swarm memory write/read through the gate** (S3):
   - A retro was stored as `fact` at `repo:` scope, accepted and verified. Its replay was stored once.
   - A decision rewrite without `expected_version` was resolved by a re-read and resubmit (2 POSTs), giving v2.
   - A decision at agent scope returned `E-POLICY` and was not stored.
   - A `global` pattern became a `review.required` proposal and was not stored.
   - A token-shaped secret returned `quarantine.secret`, and no raw token reached Postgres.
   - `memory_query` returned the fact, and `run_start_context` (`/brief`) carried decision v2, not v1.
   - With the substrate unreachable, the client returned `unavailable`.
